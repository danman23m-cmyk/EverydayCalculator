How to Calculate an Average

The average, also called the arithmetic mean, is calculated by adding all the numbers together and dividing the sum by the number of values.

Average = Sum of Numbers ÷ Number of Values

Example

Suppose you have the numbers: 10, 20, 30, and 40.

First add them together:

10 + 20 + 30 + 40 = 100

There are 4 numbers, so:

100 ÷ 4 = 25

Therefore, the average is 25.
