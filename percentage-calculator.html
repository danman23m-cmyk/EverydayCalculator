How to Calculate a Percentage

A percentage shows how much one number represents out of another. The basic percentage formula is:

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

Example

Suppose you scored 25 points out of 100.

Percentage = (25 ÷ 100) × 100 = 25%
