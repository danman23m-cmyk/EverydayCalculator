Discount Calculator

Calculate how much you save and find the final sale price after applying a percentage discount.

How to Calculate a Discount

To calculate the amount saved, multiply the original price by the discount percentage expressed as a decimal.

Discount Amount = Original Price × (Discount ÷ 100)

Then subtract the discount amount from the original price:

Sale Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Example

Suppose an item costs $100 and is on sale for 20% off.

Discount = $100 × 0.20 = $20

Sale Price = $100 − $20 = $80

You save $20 and pay $80.

How to Use This Calculator
