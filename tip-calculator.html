Tip Calculator

Calculate your tip, total bill, and cost per person when splitting a bill with friends or family.

How to Calculate a Tip

To calculate a tip, multiply the bill amount by the tip percentage converted to a decimal.

Tip = Bill Amount × (Tip Percentage ÷ 100)

To find the total bill:

Total = Bill Amount + Tip

Example

Suppose your restaurant bill is $50 and you want to leave a 20% tip.

Tip = $50 × 0.20 = $10

Total = $50 + $10 = $60

If two people split the bill:

$60 ÷ 2 = $30 per person

How to Use This Calculator

1. Enter your bill amount.

2. Enter the tip percentage you want to leave.

3. Enter the number of people splitting the bill.

4. Click Calculate Tip.

Common Tip Percentages

Common restaurant tip amounts include 15%, 18%, and 20%. The appropriate tip can vary depending on the service and situation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I tip?

A common restaurant tip is between 15% and 20%, although the appropriate amount depends on the situation and service.

How do you calculate a tip?

Multiply the bill by the tip percentage expressed as a decimal. For example, 20% is 0.20.

How do I split a bill?

Add the tip to the original bill and divide the total by the number of people.

Can I use this calculator on my phone?

Yes. The calculator works on both desktop and mobile browsers.
