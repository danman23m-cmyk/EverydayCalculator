Sales Tax Calculator

Calculate the sales tax and final price of an item. Enter the original price and tax rate below.

How to Calculate Sales Tax

Sales tax is calculated by multiplying the original price by the tax rate expressed as a decimal.

Sales Tax = Price × (Tax Rate ÷ 100)

To find the final price, add the sales tax to the original price:

Total Price = Price + Sales Tax

Example

Suppose an item costs $100 and the sales tax rate is 7.5%.

Sales Tax = $100 × 0.075 = $7.50

Total Price = $100 + $7.50 = $107.50

How to Use This Calculator

1. Enter the item's original price.

2. Enter the sales tax rate as a percentage.

3. Click Calculate Sales Tax.

The calculator will show the tax amount and final total.

Why Sales Tax Matters

Sales tax can make the final price of an item higher than its listed price. Calculating the tax before buying something can help you estimate the actual amount you will pay.
